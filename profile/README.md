## 我们是谁 · Who we are

东方之翼是一个由航空摄影爱好者组成的社群。我们拍飞机，也为"拍好一架飞机"这件事定下标准：每一张提交的作品都由人审阅，按公开的收录准则判断取舍，而不是按热度或算法。

Eastern Wing is a community of aviation photographers. We shoot aircraft, and we hold a standard for shooting them well: every submission is read by a person and judged against published criteria — never by popularity or an algorithm.

## 我们做什么 · What we build

我们运营一座航空摄影画廊。摄影师提交作品，审核员依据收录准则复核，入选作品进入画廊、机队与航空公司档案，以及「编辑之选」和「七日内热门」两个栏目。

We run an aviation photography gallery. Photographers submit work, screeners review it against the published criteria, and accepted images join the gallery, the aircraft and airline archive, and the two editorial features — Screener's Choice and 7-Day Hot.

我们看重的是真实与准确：飞机、光线、时刻，以及按下快门的那一次判断。作品的取舍因此有据可查，也有迹可循。

What we value is truth and precision — the aircraft, the light, the moment, and the judgement behind the shutter. That is why our decisions are recorded and open to question.
